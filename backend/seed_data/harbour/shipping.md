## Dispatch times
- In-stock items are dispatched within 48 hours of payment. Pre-order items ship on the date shown on the product page.
- During peak periods such as Black Friday and the Christmas cut-off, dispatch can take up to 72 hours.

## Carriers
- We ship with Australia Post and Sendle, and the carrier is chosen for you at dispatch.
- Once an order is dispatched, the tracking number and delivery updates appear under "My orders".

## Delivery areas and shipping fees
- We deliver Australia-wide. Standard shipping is free on orders over $99; orders under $99 pay an $8.00 shipping fee.
- Remote and regional postcodes, such as outback Western Australia, the Northern Territory and far north Queensland, pay an extra $15.00 regional surcharge.
- Express shipping is $12.95 and arrives in 1-2 business days in metropolitan areas.
- We do not ship internationally at the moment.
