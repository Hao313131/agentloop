# Support handbook

> Internal staff only. Synthetic demo data (SYNTHETIC).

## Goodwill authority
- A frontline agent may offer a goodwill voucher of up to $20 to a distressed customer, once per customer per month.
- Anything above $20 needs approval from the support team leader.

## Escalation
- If a customer mentions the ACCC, Fair Trading, the media or a lawyer, escalate to the support team leader within 30 minutes.

## Wording
- Never promise a refund timeframe or an amount of compensation that is not in the published policy.
