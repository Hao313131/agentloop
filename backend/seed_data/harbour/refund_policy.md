# Returns and refunds

> Synthetic demo data (SYNTHETIC). It does not reflect the policy of any real business.

## Change of mind returns
- Within 30 days of delivery you can return an item for a refund, as long as it is unused and in its original packaging.
- Personalised items, perishable food and opened underwear are excluded from change of mind returns.
- On a change of mind return the customer pays return postage. If the order included return shipping cover, the insurer reimburses that postage.
- The Australian Consumer Law guarantees apply on top of this policy and are never limited by it.

## Faulty items
- If an item is faulty or does not match its description, you can ask for a refund, repair or replacement within 90 days of delivery. Harbour & Home pays postage both ways.
- Attach photos of the problem with a faulty item claim and support reviews the claim within 24 hours.
- For a major failure the Australian Consumer Law gives you the right to choose the remedy, and that right is not limited to 90 days.

## Refund timing
- Once a refund is approved it is paid back to the original payment method.
- Card refunds take 1-3 business days to appear on your statement.
- Bank transfer refunds take 3-7 business days, depending on your bank.
- PayPal refunds are usually back in your PayPal balance within 1 business day.

## Refund approval
- Refunds of $75 or less are approved automatically.
- Refunds above $75 are reviewed by a support agent, usually within 24 hours.
- An order that already has a refund in progress, or has been refunded, cannot be refunded again.
