# Tax invoices

> Synthetic demo data (SYNTHETIC).

## Tax invoices for orders
- After an order is delivered you can request a tax invoice under "My orders > Request a tax invoice".
- The tax invoice is emailed within 1 business day, and all prices include 10% GST.

## Company invoices
- Business customers can have a company name and ABN added to the tax invoice.
- Submit the ABN and the registered business name; adding them takes 1 business day.

## Changing an invoice
- Within 30 days of issue you can change the invoice details once.
